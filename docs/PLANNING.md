# Monthly planning and historical insights

All display dates, date selectors, reporting periods and new monthly plans use the Solar Hijri calendar in both languages. ISO dates remain the wire/storage representation. Existing Gregorian budgets keep their original bounds, shown as a Solar Hijri date range.

Monthly plans support a whole-toman amount, custom categories and percentages to two decimals. Percentages total at most 100%; the rest is unassigned. Largest-remainder allocation preserves the exact total. The lower chart counts expenses and explicitly categorized transfers once, excluding income and ordinary Other transfers. Goal tracker progress is excluded.

Recurring expense templates specify a Persian due day and start/end month. Recording payment creates a normal editable expense, linked to that template and Persian month, with duplicate protection. This only records payment. Notes support search and pinning; savings goals remain available.

Investment steps record contribution amount/date and optional actual value/valuation date. Each benchmark buys at the first available close on/after each contribution, within seven days. Valuation uses a common target date (minimum latest available date among nonempty curves), with a quote at most seven days earlier. Each benchmark's actual quote date is displayed. If the common date is more than seven days stale, no current result is calculated. Missing observations are never interpolated. Aggregate benchmark returns require coverage of every step; actual returns require matching valuation dates for all steps. Returns are nominal profit / contributed amount, not annualized or XIRR.

Public feeds: TGJU daily closes for 18K gold, USD and BTC/USD; BTC is converted using historic USD/toman on/before that day within seven days. Yaghoot Agah uses actual traded closing unit prices from TSETMC, with exact fund lookup; an adjustment event disables raw history. Iranian rial feeds are divided by ten once. Unavailable prices remain unavailable. Manual CSV imports accept date,price (Solar Hijri or ISO), explicit rial/toman, source name and confirmation of comparable adjusted prices. BTC imports are already denominated in rial/toman per coin. Imports merge by date and preserve other observations.

The historical mix maximizes final value under a per-asset cap (default 40%) and minimum fund weight (default 20%). All four benchmarks must be complete. Weights are constant for every contribution, fractional units are allowed, and there is no rebalancing. Fees, spreads, taxes, custody costs and interim risk are excluded. This is hindsight, not a forecast or personal investment recommendation. The unconstrained historical maximum is the best single available benchmark.

The full .NET app stores new record kinds in its existing versioned per-user JSON ledger in SQLite. Google authenticates identity; the app's server syncs all financial data. Public price caches contain no contribution information. The hosted private Site syncs only extension portfolio snapshots through D1; manual/planning records remain browser-local there. Self-host the full .NET app with Google and HTTPS for cross-device sync of all new sections. See [Persian guide](PLANNING-fa.md) for usage and storage details.
